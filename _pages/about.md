I'm Jason Kou.

Three things take up most of my time:

**AI agents and automation.** Handing the repetitive work to agents — news scanning, content production, business process orchestration. What I care about is whether it runs reliably and what it costs, not whether the demo looks good.

**Local models.** Speech-to-text and text-to-speech on Apple Silicon, fully offline. Data stays on the machine and there's no API meter running. Open-sourced on [GitHub](https://github.com/Jason-Kou).

**Content.** Turning all of the above into videos and long-form writing. Engineering detail and the things that actually broke — not repackaged secondhand news.
