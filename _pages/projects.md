Most of what I build circles one idea: **run the AI locally and make it actually save time** — not another demo that needs a login, a subscription, and ten manual clicks.

## zhiyin — Voice Input for macOS

Hold a hotkey, talk, let go. The text appears at your cursor, in any app.

- 14 languages, **100% offline** — audio never leaves the machine
- MLX backend, near-realtime on Apple Silicon
- No account, no subscription

[GitHub →](https://github.com/Jason-Kou/zhiyin){: .btn .btn--primary}

## ExpoSnap — Trade Show Card Scanner (iOS)

The problem with collecting business cards at a show is that you still have to type them all in back at the hotel. ExpoSnap ends that.

- Detects card edges automatically, OCR pulls the contact out in one tap
- Attach a **voice memo, follow-up task, booth photo, or note** to each contact — so you remember the conversation, not just the name
- OCR and storage run on-device, so it **works with no signal**
- Export to the iPhone address book or push to your CRM

[App Store →](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/exposnap/id6752795586){: .btn .btn--primary}
[Support](https://exposnap-support.vercel.app){: .btn .btn--inverse}
